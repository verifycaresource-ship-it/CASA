Section 5: Shariah Principle and Declaration

I/We agree to participate in this general takaful scheme based on the principle of takaful and to pay the contribution based on tabarru' (donation) for the purpose of helping each other's participants. Contributions will be credited to the General Takaful Fund managed by Ihsan_care under Shariah principles.

At the end of each financial year, the Fund calculates surplus/deficit. 30% of the surplus is retained for the Fund’s viability, and 70% is distributed among participants, or credited to charity if below US$10.
